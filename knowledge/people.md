# People

<<CONFIGURE>> Replace this file with person-specific knowledge about the user
(and anyone else who shows up in their work). The layout below is a suggested
skeleton — keep what's useful, drop what isn't, add sections as you go.

This file is referenced from `meta/instructions.md`. Until you fill it in,
Claude has no durable picture of who it's working with, and the First Session
ritual will trigger on the next conversation.

## [User name / handle]

### Background
- Where they're from, what they've worked on, communities they're part of

### Current Work
- What they're focused on right now, who they collaborate with, what's in flight

### Identity & Background
- Demographics, languages, cultural context, anything that shapes how they read a situation

### Physical
- Health constraints, sensitivities, accessibility needs that affect how Claude should help (e.g. finger pain → prefer scripts over copy-paste)

### Living Situation
- Location, household, constraints that affect what's actionable

### Personality & Interests
- Hobbies, values, communication preferences, things they enjoy being challenged on

### Working Relationship
- How they want Claude to behave: tone, formality, what to avoid, what they explicitly *don't* want

## [Other people]

- Partners, collaborators, recurring contacts. One subsection per person, only what's relevant to the work.

## Terminology Map

- Names, aliases, projects, in-group jargon — anything where getting the wrong term will confuse a conversation.
